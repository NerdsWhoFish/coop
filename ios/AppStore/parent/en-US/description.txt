Cooper The Cop is the parent side of Coop, a self-hosted way to decide what your family can watch on YouTube.

Approve channels, block what does not belong, handle requests, and tune each child's feed from one app.
Coop keeps the policy and viewing history on a server you control.

Cooper The Cop requires a Coop server and the companion Cooper Watch app.
It does not include videos or a built-in catalog.
Your family supplies and approves every channel.
